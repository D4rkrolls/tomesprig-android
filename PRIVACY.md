# Privacy

Quillfold's prototype does not include analytics, advertising, account, or crash-reporting services. It does not access the device filesystem or request permissions for contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Android backup is disabled.

The app can open publisher pages in the user's browser. In that case, the selected publisher receives the browser request in the usual way. Feed entries in this version are sample content; RSS fetching and custom feed persistence are not implemented.
